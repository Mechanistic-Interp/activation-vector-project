CARROT CAKE INSTRUCTIONS

========================================
INGREDIENTS
========================================

FOR THE CARROT CAKE:
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups vegetable oil or canola oil
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 3 cups grated carrots (about 4-5 medium carrots)
- 1 cup crushed pineapple, drained (optional but recommended)
- 1 cup chopped walnuts or pecans (optional)
- 1 cup sweetened shredded coconut (optional)

FOR THE CREAM CHEESE FROSTING:
- 16 oz (2 packages) cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
- 4 cups powdered sugar, sifted
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

========================================
INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

PREPARING THE CAKE:

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round 
   cake pans, or line them with parchment paper. Alternatively, you can use 
   a 9x13-inch pan.

2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, nutmeg, 
   and salt. Set aside.

3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the oil and sugar until well combined.

4. Add the eggs one at a time, whisking well after each addition.

5. Stir in the vanilla extract.

6. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined. 
   Do not overmix.

7. Fold in the grated carrots, drained pineapple (if using), nuts (if using), 
   and coconut (if using). Stir until evenly distributed throughout the batter.

8. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans (or pour into the 
   9x13-inch pan).

9. Bake for 30-35 minutes for round pans (or 40-45 minutes for a 9x13 pan), 
   until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

10. Remove from oven and let the cakes cool in the pans for 10-15 minutes.

11. Turn the cakes out onto wire racks to cool completely before frosting 
    (about 1-2 hours).

MAKING THE CREAM CHEESE FROSTING:

1. In a large bowl, beat the softened cream cheese and butter together until 
   smooth and creamy (about 2-3 minutes).

2. Gradually add the powdered sugar, one cup at a time, beating well after 
   each addition.

3. Add the vanilla extract and a pinch of salt. Beat until light and fluffy.

4. If the frosting is too thick, add 1-2 tablespoons of milk or cream. If too 
   thin, add more powdered sugar.

ASSEMBLING THE CAKE (FOR LAYER CAKE):

1. Place one cake layer on a serving plate.

2. Spread about 1 to 1 1/2 cups of cream cheese frosting over the top.

3. Place the second cake layer on top.

4. Frost the top and sides of the entire cake with the remaining frosting.

5. If desired, decorate with additional chopped nuts, shredded carrots, or 
   a sprinkle of cinnamon on top.

6. Refrigerate the cake for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the 
   frosting set.

FOR SHEET CAKE:

1. Once the cake has cooled completely in the pan, spread the cream cheese 
   frosting evenly over the top.

2. Decorate as desired.

3. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before cutting and serving.

========================================
TIPS FOR SUCCESS
========================================

CARROT PREPARATION:
- Use the fine holes on a box grater for the best texture
- Freshly grated carrots are best - avoid pre-shredded bagged carrots as 
  they're often too dry
- Pat the grated carrots with paper towels if they seem very wet

INGREDIENT TIPS:
- Room temperature eggs mix better and create a more uniform batter
- Make sure cream cheese and butter are fully softened for smooth frosting
- Sift powdered sugar to avoid lumps in the frosting
- If using pineapple, drain it very well and pat dry with paper towels

BAKING TIPS:
- Don't overmix the batter - mix just until combined
- Check cakes at the minimum baking time to avoid overbaking
- Cakes are done when they spring back when lightly touched
- Let cakes cool completely before frosting or the frosting will melt

STORAGE:
- Store frosted carrot cake covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days
- Bring to room temperature 30 minutes before serving for best flavor
- Unfrosted cake layers can be wrapped tightly and frozen for up to 3 months
- Frosted cake can be frozen for up to 1 month

VARIATIONS:
- Add 1/2 cup raisins for extra sweetness
- Use a mix of walnuts and pecans
- Add 1 teaspoon of ground ginger for extra spice
- Top with candied carrots or edible flowers for decoration
- Make as cupcakes: fill liners 2/3 full and bake for 18-22 minutes

========================================

Prep Time: 25 minutes
Bake Time: 30-35 minutes
Cooling & Frosting Time: 2 hours
Total Time: About 3 hours

Servings: 12-16 slices

Enjoy your homemade Carrot Cake!
