GERMAN CHOCOLATE CAKE RECIPE

========================================
ABOUT THIS CAKE
========================================

German Chocolate Cake is a classic American dessert featuring layers of rich 
chocolate cake topped with a signature coconut-pecan frosting. Despite its 
name, it's not from Germany - it's named after Samuel German, who developed 
a type of dark baking chocolate for Baker's Chocolate Company in 1852.

========================================
INGREDIENTS LIST
========================================

CHOCOLATE CAKE LAYERS:
- 4 ounces German's sweet chocolate (or semi-sweet baking chocolate)
- 1/2 cup boiling water
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 2 cups white sugar
- 4 eggs, separated (yolks and whites divided)
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup buttermilk

COCONUT-PECAN FROSTING:
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 1 cup white sugar
- 3 egg yolks, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 1/3 cups sweetened flaked coconut
- 1 cup pecans, chopped

========================================
EQUIPMENT NEEDED
========================================

- Three 9-inch round cake pans
- Parchment paper
- Mixing bowls (large and medium)
- Electric mixer or stand mixer
- Whisk
- Spatula
- Medium saucepan
- Wire cooling racks
- Toothpicks for testing

========================================
STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

STEP 1: PREPARE YOUR WORKSPACE
- Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
- Grease three 9-inch round cake pans with butter or cooking spray
- Line the bottoms with parchment paper circles
- Lightly flour the pans and tap out excess

STEP 2: MELT THE CHOCOLATE
- Place chocolate pieces in a small heatproof bowl
- Pour 1/2 cup boiling water over the chocolate
- Stir until completely melted and smooth
- Set aside to cool to room temperature

STEP 3: COMBINE DRY INGREDIENTS
- In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt
- Set aside

STEP 4: CREAM BUTTER AND SUGAR
- In a large mixing bowl, beat butter until creamy (about 1 minute)
- Gradually add sugar and beat on medium-high speed for 3-5 minutes
- The mixture should be light, fluffy, and pale in color

STEP 5: ADD EGG YOLKS
- Add egg yolks one at a time, beating well after each addition
- Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed
- Mix in the cooled melted chocolate and vanilla extract

STEP 6: ALTERNATE WET AND DRY INGREDIENTS
- Add about 1/3 of the flour mixture to the batter and mix on low speed
- Add half of the buttermilk and mix until combined
- Repeat: flour, buttermilk, flour
- Mix only until ingredients are incorporated - don't overmix

STEP 7: BEAT EGG WHITES
- In a clean, dry bowl, beat egg whites with clean beaters
- Beat until stiff peaks form (the peaks should stand up straight)
- This takes about 3-4 minutes on high speed

STEP 8: FOLD IN EGG WHITES
- Add 1/3 of the egg whites to the batter and gently fold in
- Add remaining egg whites in two more additions
- Use a folding motion to keep the batter light and airy
- Fold just until no white streaks remain

STEP 9: BAKE THE CAKES
- Divide batter evenly among the three prepared pans
- Tap pans gently on the counter to release air bubbles
- Bake for 30-35 minutes
- Cakes are done when a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
- The tops should spring back when lightly touched

STEP 10: COOL THE CAKES
- Let cakes cool in pans for 10 minutes on wire racks
- Run a knife around the edges to loosen
- Invert onto wire racks and remove parchment paper
- Allow cakes to cool completely (at least 1 hour)

STEP 11: MAKE THE FROSTING
- In a medium saucepan, combine evaporated milk, sugar, egg yolks, and butter
- Place over medium heat
- Stir constantly with a wooden spoon or whisk
- Cook for 12-15 minutes until mixture thickens and turns golden
- The mixture should coat the back of a spoon
- Remove from heat immediately when thickened

STEP 12: FINISH THE FROSTING
- Stir in vanilla extract
- Add coconut and chopped pecans, mixing well
- Let frosting cool to room temperature, stirring occasionally
- It will continue to thicken as it cools (about 45 minutes)

STEP 13: ASSEMBLE THE CAKE
- Place first cake layer on your serving plate or cake stand
- Spread approximately 1 cup of frosting evenly over the top
- Leave a small border around the edge
- Place second layer on top and add another cup of frosting
- Add the final layer and spread remaining frosting on top
- DO NOT frost the sides - this is traditional for German Chocolate Cake
- The chocolate layers should be visible from the sides

STEP 14: SERVE
- Let the assembled cake sit for 30 minutes to 1 hour before slicing
- Cut with a sharp knife, wiping it clean between slices
- Serve at room temperature for best flavor

========================================
HELPFUL TIPS & TRICKS
========================================

INGREDIENT TIPS:
- Use German's sweet chocolate if you can find it for authentic flavor
- Room temperature ingredients blend more smoothly
- Fresh baking soda is important for proper rise
- Full-fat buttermilk gives the best texture

BAKING TIPS:
- Don't open the oven door during the first 25 minutes of baking
- Oven temperatures can vary - start checking at 30 minutes
- Cakes should pull away slightly from the pan edges when done
- If tops are browning too quickly, tent loosely with foil

FROSTING TIPS:
- Stir the frosting constantly to prevent scorching
- If frosting seems too thin, let it cool longer
- If it's too thick, stir in a tablespoon of evaporated milk
- The frosting firms up significantly as it cools
- Toast the pecans beforehand for extra flavor (optional)

ASSEMBLY TIPS:
- Make sure cake layers are completely cool before frosting
- Use a cake turntable if you have one for easier assembly
- A serrated knife works best for leveling uneven layers
- Brush off any loose crumbs before adding frosting

STORAGE:
- Cover loosely with plastic wrap or store in a cake keeper
- Refrigerate for up to 5 days
- Bring to room temperature 30 minutes before serving
- Frosting keeps the cake moist for several days
- Can be frozen (unfrosted layers) for up to 3 months

MAKE-AHEAD OPTIONS:
- Bake cake layers 1-2 days ahead, wrap tightly, and refrigerate
- Make frosting up to 3 days ahead and refrigerate
- Assemble the cake up to 24 hours before serving

========================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
========================================

PROBLEM: Cake is dense and heavy
SOLUTION: Make sure egg whites are beaten to stiff peaks and folded in gently

PROBLEM: Cake sinks in the middle
SOLUTION: Don't open oven too early; ensure baking soda is fresh

PROBLEM: Frosting is runny
SOLUTION: Cook longer until it coats a spoon; let cool completely

PROBLEM: Cake is dry
SOLUTION: Don't overbake; check at 30 minutes; store covered

PROBLEM: Layers are uneven
SOLUTION: Measure batter with a scale for even distribution

========================================
SERVING SUGGESTIONS
========================================

- Serve with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream
- Pairs wonderfully with hot coffee or cold milk
- Garnish with extra toasted coconut or pecans
- Perfect for birthdays, holidays, or special occasions

========================================

Prep Time: 30 minutes
Baking Time: 35 minutes
Cooling & Assembly Time: 2 hours
Total Time: Approximately 3 hours

Servings: 12-16 slices

Enjoy your homemade German Chocolate Cake!
