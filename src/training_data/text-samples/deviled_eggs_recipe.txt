DEVILED EGGS RECIPE

========================================
INGREDIENTS
========================================

BASIC RECIPE:
- 12 large eggs
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Paprika for garnish
- Fresh chives or parsley for garnish (optional)

========================================
INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

STEP 1: HARD BOIL THE EGGS

1. Place eggs in a single layer in a large saucepan or pot.

2. Cover eggs with cold water by about 1 inch.

3. Place pot on stove over high heat and bring to a rolling boil.

4. Once water reaches a full boil, remove pot from heat and cover with a lid.

5. Let eggs sit in the hot water for 10-12 minutes.

6. While eggs are cooking, prepare an ice bath: fill a large bowl with ice 
   and cold water.

7. After 10-12 minutes, use a slotted spoon to transfer eggs to the ice bath.

8. Let eggs cool in ice bath for at least 5 minutes. This stops the cooking 
   process and makes peeling easier.

STEP 2: PEEL THE EGGS

1. Gently tap each egg on a hard surface to crack the shell all over.

2. Roll the egg between your hands to loosen the shell.

3. Start peeling from the wider end where the air pocket is located.

4. Peel under cold running water if needed - this helps remove stubborn bits 
   of shell.

5. Rinse peeled eggs to remove any remaining shell fragments.

6. Pat eggs dry with paper towels.

STEP 3: PREPARE THE FILLING

1. Slice each peeled egg in half lengthwise.

2. Carefully remove the yolks and place them in a medium bowl.

3. Arrange the egg white halves on a serving platter.

4. Mash the yolks with a fork until they're crumbly and smooth.

5. Add the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, and vinegar to the mashed yolks.

6. Mix thoroughly until the mixture is smooth and creamy.

7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Start with 1/4 teaspoon salt and 
   1/8 teaspoon pepper, then adjust as needed.

8. For extra smooth filling, you can press the mixture through a fine mesh 
   sieve or blend briefly in a food processor.

STEP 4: FILL THE EGGS

METHOD 1 - Using a Spoon:
1. Scoop the yolk mixture into each egg white half.
2. Smooth the top with the back of the spoon.

METHOD 2 - Using a Piping Bag (Recommended for presentation):
1. Transfer the yolk mixture to a piping bag fitted with a large star tip.
2. Pipe the filling into each egg white half, creating a decorative swirl.
3. If you don't have a piping bag, use a plastic zip-top bag with one corner 
   cut off.

STEP 5: GARNISH AND SERVE

1. Sprinkle each deviled egg with paprika for classic presentation.

2. Add additional garnishes if desired (see variations below).

3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to let 
   flavors meld.

4. Serve chilled on a deviled egg platter or regular serving plate.

========================================
TIPS FOR PERFECT DEVILED EGGS
========================================

BOILING TIPS:
- Use eggs that are at least one week old - they peel easier than very fresh 
  eggs
- Add 1 teaspoon of baking soda to the water to make peeling easier
- Don't overcook eggs or you'll get a green ring around the yolk
- The ice bath is crucial for easy peeling

FILLING TIPS:
- For extra creamy filling, add 1-2 tablespoons of sour cream
- Adjust consistency by adding more mayo if too thick, or more yolk if too thin
- Make filling ahead and refrigerate in a piping bag for easy assembly
- Taste the filling before stuffing and adjust seasonings

PRESENTATION TIPS:
- To keep eggs from sliding around, slice a tiny bit off the bottom of each 
  white to create a flat base
- Use a deviled egg carrier or plate with indentations to keep eggs stable
- Garnish just before serving so toppings stay fresh and crisp
- Arrange on a bed of lettuce for an attractive presentation

STORAGE:
- Store deviled eggs covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days
- Keep eggs and filling separate if making more than 4 hours ahead
- Don't leave deviled eggs at room temperature for more than 2 hours
- Transport in a cooler with ice packs for picnics or parties

========================================
FLAVOR VARIATIONS
========================================

CLASSIC VARIATIONS:

SOUTHERN STYLE:
- Add 2 tablespoons sweet pickle relish
- Use yellow mustard instead of Dijon
- Top with a slice of dill pickle

BACON DEVILED EGGS:
- Mix 3-4 strips of crumbled crispy bacon into the filling
- Garnish with additional bacon crumbles and chives

SPICY DEVILED EGGS:
- Add 1-2 teaspoons sriracha or hot sauce to the filling
- Garnish with sliced jalapeños or cayenne pepper
- Mix in a dash of chipotle powder

DILL PICKLE DEVILED EGGS:
- Replace vinegar with pickle juice
- Add 2 tablespoons finely chopped dill pickles
- Garnish with fresh dill and a pickle slice

AVOCADO DEVILED EGGS:
- Mash 1 ripe avocado into the yolk mixture
- Add a squeeze of lime juice
- Garnish with cilantro and paprika

GOURMET VARIATIONS:

SMOKED SALMON:
- Add 2 oz finely chopped smoked salmon to filling
- Mix in 1 tablespoon capers and fresh dill
- Garnish with a small piece of salmon and dill sprig

TRUFFLE DEVILED EGGS:
- Add 1 teaspoon truffle oil to the filling
- Garnish with shaved parmesan and black pepper

BUFFALO STYLE:
- Mix 2 tablespoons buffalo wing sauce into filling
- Top with crumbled blue cheese and celery leaves

MEDITERRANEAN:
- Add 2 tablespoons chopped kalamata olives
- Mix in 1 tablespoon chopped sun-dried tomatoes
- Top with feta cheese and fresh oregano

CRAB DEVILED EGGS:
- Fold 1/2 cup lump crab meat into filling
- Add a dash of Old Bay seasoning
- Garnish with more crab and paprika

========================================
COMMON PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS
========================================

PROBLEM: Eggs are hard to peel
SOLUTION: Use older eggs (1 week+) and ice bath immediately after cooking

PROBLEM: Green ring around yolk
SOLUTION: Don't overcook eggs; use timer and ice bath

PROBLEM: Filling is too dry
SOLUTION: Add more mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon at a time

PROBLEM: Filling is too runny
SOLUTION: Add more mashed yolk or breadcrumbs to thicken

PROBLEM: Eggs slide around on plate
SOLUTION: Trim bottom of whites slightly or use deviled egg platter

PROBLEM: Yolks fall apart when cutting eggs
SOLUTION: Use a sharp knife and wipe clean between each cut

========================================
MAKE-AHEAD INSTRUCTIONS
========================================

UP TO 2 DAYS AHEAD:
- Hard boil and peel eggs
- Store whole peeled eggs in water in the refrigerator
- Change water daily

UP TO 1 DAY AHEAD:
- Prepare filling and store in piping bag or covered bowl
- Cut eggs in half and store separately
- Fill eggs 2-4 hours before serving

DAY OF SERVING:
- Fill eggs and arrange on platter
- Cover with plastic wrap
- Refrigerate until 30 minutes before serving
- Add garnishes just before serving
